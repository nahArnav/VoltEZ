## 1. Problem Definition & Market Realities

Electric Vehicle (EV) adoption in India is accelerating rapidly across two-wheelers (2W), four-wheelers (4W), and commercial fleets. However, EV drivers and charging hosts face critical friction points:

1. **Charger Reliability & "Phantom" Availability**: Drivers arrive at charging stations only to find ports broken, blocked by ICE vehicles, or occupied with lengthy unpredicted queues.
2. **Fragmented Ecosystem**: Drivers must switch between 10+ disjointed CPO (Charge Point Operator) apps (Tata Power, Jio-bp, Statiq, Zeon, Ather) with separate wallets and no unified real-time telemetry.
3. **Underutilized Private Infrastructure**: Captive commercial hosts (hotels, IT parks, malls, apartments) lack dynamic pricing and visibility tools to monetize idle charging hardware.
4. **Range & Route Anxiety on Inter-city Highways**: High-speed highway corridors (such as the Pune-Mumbai Expressway) experience severe bottlenecking during peak hours.

## 5. Go-To-Market (GTM) 90-Day Execution Plan

### Phase 1: The Pune Epicenter (Days 1–30)
- **Target Territory**: Hinjewadi Tech Park, Baner, Wakad, Kothrud, and Viman Nagar.
- **Supply Blitz**: Onboard 50 commercial and private host chargers (IT parks, commercial societies, hotel parking).
- **Demand Seeding**: Partner with local Pune EV user clubs (Tata EV Owners Club, MG EV Pune Community) and commercial EV drivers.
- **Key Metrics**: 1,000 active app users, 250 completed charging sessions, >95% slot reservation fulfillment rate.

### Phase 2: Pune–Mumbai Highway Corridor (Days 31–60)
- **Target Territory**: Pune–Mumbai Expressway, Lonavala, Talegaon, and Navi Mumbai entry points.
- **Highway Hub Integration**: Partner with food courts and fuel stations to deploy fast-charging reservations.
- **Key Metrics**: 5,000 active drivers, 1,500 inter-city bookings, 0% double-booking incidents.

### Phase 3: Regional Scale & Fleet Partnerships (Days 61–90)
- **Target Territory**: Mumbai Metropolitan Region (MMR), Nashik, and Kolhapur.
- **Enterprise Pilot**: Onboard 2 last-mile delivery fleet operators (e-commerce / grocery delivery).
- **Key Metrics**: ₹500,000 monthly Gross Merchandise Value (GMV), 15,000 monthly bookings, 150 live hosts.
