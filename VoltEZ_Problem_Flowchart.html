What's the current gap in the system?
🚫

No demand-supply intelligence on the network

Charging networks run today without dynamic pricing or predictive congestion alerts — so supply and demand are never balanced. Drivers arrive to find busy or idle stations, while operators can't anticipate demand, manage congestion, or price intelligently.

Missing: Dynamic Pricing · Predictive Congestion Alerts
What evidence validates the need?
📈

Rapid EV Adoption

Growing EV usage is steadily increasing demand for reliable, accessible public and semi-public charging infrastructure — supply is struggling to keep pace.

😤

User & Operator Pain Points

Drivers face daily uncertainty around charger availability, while operators lack effective tools for demand forecasting, congestion management, and pricing.
